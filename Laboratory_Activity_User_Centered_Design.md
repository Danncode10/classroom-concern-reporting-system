# Laboratory Activity in Human Computer Interaction

## User Centered Design

### Title

**Designing a Student-Friendly Classroom Concern Reporting System**

## Learning Objectives

At the end of the activity, the students should be able to:

1. Identify the needs, problems, and preferences of users.
2. Create a simple user persona based on gathered information.
3. Design a user interface that is easy to understand and use.
4. Conduct user testing and gather feedback.
5. Improve a design based on the comments and experiences of users.
6. Explain the importance of involving users in the design process.

## Background

User Centered Design is an approach that places the users at the center of the design process. The designer first studies the users, their needs, abilities, experiences, and difficulties before creating a product or system.

In this activity, your group will design a simple classroom concern reporting system. The system will allow students to report concerns such as broken chairs, damaged electric fans, missing equipment, electrical problems, or cleanliness issues.

## Grouping

Form a group composed of four to two members.

Each member should be assigned one of the following roles:

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Group Leader | Manages the activity and ensures that all members participate. |
| Interviewer | Asks questions and gathers information from the target user. |
| Recorder | Writes the answers and important observations during the interview and testing. |
| Designer | Leads the creation of the interface design. |
| Presenter | Explains the group output to the class. |

Members may help one another even if they have assigned roles.

## Materials

Prepare the following materials:

1. Mobile phone
2. Laptop
3. iPad or tablet

## Situation

Many students experience classroom concerns such as broken chairs, damaged electric fans, missing classroom equipment, leaking roofs, electrical problems, or unclean surroundings.

In some situations, students do not know where, how, or to whom they should report these concerns. The school plans to create a digital classroom concern reporting system.

Your group has been selected to design the interface of the system.

## General Instructions

1. Work with your assigned group.
2. Read and understand the given situation.
3. Select one student who will serve as your target user.
4. Interview the target user to identify their needs and difficulties.
5. Create a user persona based on the interview.
6. Identify the important features of the system.
7. Draw the first version of the interface.
8. Ask another group to test your design.
9. Record the comments and suggestions of the testers.
10. Improve the design based on the feedback.
11. Present the final design to the class.

## Part 1: Understanding the User

### Student Interview

Select one student who will serve as the target user. Ask the following questions:

1. What classroom problems do you commonly experience?
2. How do you currently report classroom problems?
3. Who do you usually approach when there is a classroom concern?
4. What difficulties do you experience when reporting a concern?
5. What information should be included in a classroom concern report?
6. Would you prefer to report using a mobile phone, computer, or paper form?
7. Would you like to receive updates about your report?
8. What features would make the system easy to use?
9. What words, buttons, or symbols would be easy for students to understand?
10. What concerns do you have about using the system?

## Design Guidelines

Your interface should have:

1. Clear instructions
2. Readable text
3. Proper spacing
4. Simple words
5. Familiar symbols
6. Visible buttons
7. Consistent labels
8. Easy navigation
9. Organized information
10. Appropriate text size
