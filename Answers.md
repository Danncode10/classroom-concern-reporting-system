## Part 1: Understanding the User

### Student Interview Answers

1. **What classroom problems do you commonly experience?**  
   I commonly experience broken chairs, weak or damaged electric fans, missing extension wires, dirty classrooms, and sometimes outlets that are not working.

2. **How do you currently report classroom problems?**  
   In NVSU, we do not really have a specific system or formal way to report classroom problems. Usually, we just tell our professor about the concern, then the professor is the one who reports it or takes action.

3. **Who do you usually approach when there is a classroom concern?**  
   I usually approach the professor only because they are the ones who know what to do or who to contact about the classroom concern.

4. **What difficulties do you experience when reporting a concern?**  
   The process is unclear. Sometimes I do not know who is responsible, and I am not sure if the concern was already received or if someone is working on it.

5. **What information should be included in a classroom concern report?**  
   The report should include the classroom or location, type of concern, short description, date, name or student number of the reporter, and a photo if possible.

6. **Would you prefer to report using a mobile phone, computer, or paper form?**  
   I prefer using a mobile phone because it is faster and easier to access while I am inside the classroom.

7. **Would you like to receive updates about your report?**  
   Yes, I would like to receive updates so I know if the concern was received, being checked, or already resolved.

8. **What features would make the system easy to use?**  
   Simple buttons, a short reporting form, dropdown choices for the type of concern, photo upload, and a status tracker would make the system easy to use.

9. **What words, buttons, or symbols would be easy for students to understand?**  
   Buttons like "Report Concern," "Submit," "Upload Photo," "Check Status," and "Back" are easy to understand. Simple icons like a camera, check mark, warning sign, and search icon would also help.

10. **What concerns do you have about using the system?**  
    My concern is privacy. I want to make sure my student information is safe and that reports will not be ignored. It would also be better if the people responsible for managing and improving the school have their own accounts where they can check student concerns, update the status, and make sure the reports are actually taken into action.

Our target user needs a way to report classroom concerns quickly and clearly because the current reporting process is unclear, informal, and does not always provide updates about whether the concern has been received or resolved.
